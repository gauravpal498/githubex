Our approach

1. Discover Weeks 1–2

We interview your team, map your systems and data, and agree on what success looks like.

  • Stakeholder interviews
  • Systems and data inventory
  • Success metrics everyone signs off

2. Design Weeks 2–3

We propose a solution scoped to deliver value fast, starting with one quick win.

  • Architecture and scope
  • A first quick win, chosen together
  • A delivery plan with milestones

3. Deliver 4–12 weeks

We build in two-week sprints, with a working demo at the end of every sprint.

  • Two-week sprints
  • A demo every sprint
  • Your feedback built into the next sprint

4. Enable Final 2 weeks

We train your team so the work lasts after we leave.

  • Hands-on training sessions
  • Documentation your team can maintain
  • 30 days of support after handover
